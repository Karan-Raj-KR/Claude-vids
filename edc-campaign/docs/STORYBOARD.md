# KARAN RAJ KR — EDC campaign film · Storyboard

30.0 s · 60 fps · 1800 frames · 1920×1080 + 1080×1920 · soundtrack 120 BPM (1 beat = 30 frames). Every cut lands on a beat.

## Visual identity

- **The Box.** The film has one recurring shape: a vermilion square, the box you tick on a ballot. It is the click target in the hook. It becomes the founder tag, then the product window, then the winning dot among 121, then the crowd, then the frame for each pledge number, and finally the ballot box that gets ticked.
- **Palette (5):** Ink `#111111`, Paper `#F2EDE3`, Vermilion `#FF4A1C`, Stone `#8E887C`, Graphite `#262626`. Flat fills only, with no gradients, glow or stock "tech" textures.
- **Type (2):** *Archivo* (variable width and weight). Expanded Black is used for headlines and the name, and the regular width for supporting lines. *JetBrains Mono* is used for labels, kickers and UI data.
- **Motion language:** springs with no bounce, mask-reveals that rise from a baseline, vermilion highlight blocks that wipe left to right, a slow camera push inside each scene, the box morphing across cuts, and a cursor that clicks on the beat.
- **Sound:** an original synthesized track (kick, clap, hats, bass, chord stabs, risers, UI clicks), written for this film, so it is royalty-free.
